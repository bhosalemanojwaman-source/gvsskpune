OBJECTIVE
One of the social institutions of village Kumbhad has been organizing its activities in Pune and PCMC area of Pune District. It aims at creating cohesive family group and enriching the healthy relation ship amongst the families of Kumbhad people residing in this area. It was established in the year 1992 under the able leadership of Late Capt. Dajirao Baburao Bhosale.
