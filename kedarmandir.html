SHREE KEDAR MANDIR

There exists a historical Temple of Gram Devata "SHREE KEDAR KALKAI" in Kumbhad - which was erected in the 18th Century . However as it was erected with the locally available materials like Sag Wood, Stone Pillars and roof covered with earthen tiles (Manglore tiles) the upkeep of that Temple became difficult day by day due to its wear and tear due to the adverse climatic conditions of the Konkan region. Thus the Senior village leaders put up a proposal of erecting a new temple in front of the young generation villagers who are residing in the cities like Mumbai and Pune for employment and thus the enthusiastic group of villagers came forward and form a committee named as "SHRE KEDAR MANDIR IMARAT JIRNODDHAR NIDHI SAMITI " which has representatives of villagers from Mumbai, Pune and also the village itself.

The Committee decided to contribute funds voluntarily from each of the villagers, well wishers for which a Bank Account in SBI, Byculla Mumbai Branch was opened. In order to have financial discipline and control over the funds all the money that was collected by the volunteers was routed through this account . Within a span of two years the village volunteers could raise the funds to the tune of Rs.ONE CRORE.

Mr Ravindra Patil, a well known Architect from Chiplun was assigned with the design and implementation of the project under his supervision. Mr Anil Mistry who is known Builder for his expertise in erection of Temples within Ratnagiri District was entrusted the work of Mandir erection.

BHOOMI POOJAN AND MANDIR INAUGURATION

On 6th January 2012 the Bhoomi Pooja of the Mandir Wastu was performed at the hands of the Sr Villagers and to our surprise the entire Mandir was erected within a period of 15 months, much before the targeted date of completion i.e. December 2013. This was possible only because of the efforts put in by our volunteers in raising funds and entrusting the Committee Members in doing their job of coordination with the builder and Architect.

The Shree Kedar Mandir was inaugurated at the blessings of Swami Swarupanand Sarswati,Phulgaon, Alandi.
on 19th May 2013 and 20th May 2013.
